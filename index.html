Rules of the road

Stop and get medical help for chest pain or pressure, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, or sharp joint pain.
  • Adjust the seat and pads on each machine first. Staff at Planet Fitness will help.
  • Push for about 2 seconds, return for 3. Breathe out on the effort. Never hold your breath.
  • Left shoulder: on chest press and row, go light, stay pain-free, stop if it pinches.
  • Add 5 lbs only when 12 reps feel easy.
  • Walk 10–15 minutes on off days to build stamina.
